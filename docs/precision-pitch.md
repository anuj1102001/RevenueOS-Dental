# Precision Dental Clinic, Dubai — RevenueOS pitch package

Draft for Anuj M · 19 September 2026 · Not sent to the clinic

## The offer

Keep your current website and brand. Test whether a clearer enquiry handoff and visible follow-up queue help your team respond consistently and track what happens next.

Precision already has an established website, service information, patient stories and a chat widget. We have not audited its existing software, response times or conversion performance. Do not tell the clinic it is losing leads or that RevenueOS replaces a capability it already has.

## A 90-second demonstration

**0–15 seconds — Set the context**

“Your website already tells patients about your team and care. I’ve built an independent concept for what happens after someone asks a question: how that enquiry reaches staff, gets a next action, and has its outcome recorded.”

**15–35 seconds — Show the public walkthrough**

Open https://revenue-os-dental.vercel.app/#workflow. Select Maya’s clear-aligner enquiry, then click Capture sample enquiry.

“This tour uses fictional data. The handoff keeps the visitor’s interest and preferred callback time together, so staff have context.”

**35–55 seconds — Show follow-up and outcome**

Click Set a sample follow-up, then Record sample booking. Restart and show Keep following up if asked.

“A member of staff owns the next action and records the outcome. This tour is a simulation: it does not send messages or book a calendar appointment.”

**55–75 seconds — Show the working system**

Use the saved-enquiry form with fictional details. In a separate, already signed-in browser tab, open the owner dashboard. Refresh, open the new enquiry, save a status and note, then refresh again.

“This separate flow is working: the form saves an enquiry, and the protected dashboard saves staff updates. Automated messaging and calendar integration are not included in the current demo.”

**75–90 seconds — Ask for discovery, not a replacement project**

“Would a short walkthrough with your practice manager be useful? First I’d like to understand your existing enquiry process. If there’s a gap worth testing, we could agree a small pilot alongside your current website.”

### AI demonstration gate

Only call the concierge live AI after its endpoint returns `mode: ai` and factual, pricing, clinical-boundary and booking-limit tests pass. Until then, describe it as preset demo navigation. Do not show a failed integration as a working capability.

## Proposed pilot — commercial draft, not agreed

- Duration: 14 operational days after setup and acceptance checks; preparation time is separate.
- Footprint: one clinic, one agreed website enquiry source, one named clinic owner.
- Proposed fixed fee: AED 1,500 for setup and the pilot. This is Anuj’s draft asking price, not a market benchmark or accepted quote. Confirm effort and scope before sending it.
- Third-party AI, hosting and messaging charges: excluded; agree a separate cap in writing before enabling paid services. No messaging service is included in this pilot scope.
- Renewal: none automatically. Review results and quote ongoing support separately.
- Exit: stop new intake, provide an agreed export of the clinic’s pilot records, remove the integration and follow the agreed retention/deletion process.

### Included, subject to clinic approval

1. Configure a branded enquiry experience using approved clinic copy and service information.
2. Connect one agreed enquiry entry point to the protected staff workflow. The clinic’s existing web provider must approve and support any website insertion or handoff.
3. Capture contact preference with appropriate consent, treatment interest and the next staff action.
4. Provide status changes, notes and follow-up dates in the dashboard.
5. Train the named owner and provide a short outcome review.
6. If AI is enabled, limit it to approved service navigation and handoff; configure usage limits and test the boundaries before visitor access.

### Excluded

Website replacement; diagnosis or treatment recommendations; automatic appointment confirmation; WhatsApp, SMS or email campaigns; calendar/CRM integration; payments; clinical records; a promise of higher revenue or more bookings. Each requires separate scoping where applicable.

### Before any real-patient pilot

Agree clinic ownership and access, approved content, what data may be collected, consent wording, hosting/provider arrangements, retention, and the staff response process. Replace single-owner demo authentication with appropriate individual staff access if more than one person will use it. Add durable abuse/usage limits before exposing paid AI publicly. These are readiness gates, not features claimed to be complete today.

## Measure the pilot honestly

Use a simple agreed pilot log for timestamps and outcomes; the current dashboard does not automatically measure first-response time or send reminders.

| Measure | Definition | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enquiries received | Eligible, non-test, non-spam enquiries in the agreed channel | Saved enquiry records plus documented exclusions |
| First staff response | Time from enquiry creation to the first human response; report median and unanswered count | Staff-maintained response timestamps |
| Follow-up completion | Due follow-up actions completed divided by all actions due during the period | Staff-maintained action log |
| Booking outcome | Enquiries with a clinic-confirmed, staff-recorded booking divided by eligible enquiries | Clinic confirmation and recorded status |

Compare the same channel and definitions with an agreed baseline where records exist. If there is no usable baseline, treat the pilot as a usability and measurement trial. Report sample size and unresolved enquiries. Fourteen days and small volumes cannot establish causal improvement; do not attribute all bookings to RevenueOS or invent uplift.

4. What records can establish a baseline without exposing unnecessary patient information?
5. Who controls the website and approves a small integration?
